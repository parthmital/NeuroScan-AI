\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
\item Nine CNN, efficient CNN, and transformer models are benchmarked on brain MRI.
\item Perceptual hashing removes near-duplicate multiclass MRI samples before splitting.
\item CoaTNet-0 and EfficientNetB0 reach 99.59\% binary detection accuracy.
\item InceptionV3 and Swin Base reach 98.58\% multiclass classification accuracy.
\item EfficientNetB0 gives strong accuracy with only 5.3 million parameters.
\end{itemize}
