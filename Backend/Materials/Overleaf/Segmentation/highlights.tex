\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
\item Six 3D CNN and transformer segmentation architectures are benchmarked on BraTS.
\item UNet achieves the highest verified mean Dice score of 0.7999.
\item VNet offers the best parameter and training time efficiency.
\item Transformer models underperform CNNs under the ten-epoch training budget.
\item Per-region Dice shows whole tumour segmentation is consistently easiest.
\end{itemize}
