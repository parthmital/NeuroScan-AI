\section*{S6. Complete efficiency measurements}

Tables~S9 and S10 give the complete efficiency measurements for each task, of which the main-text efficiency table reports the paired subset. Training used dual NVIDIA Tesla T4 graphics processing units, except the binary InceptionV3 run, which executed on a single device and whose training time is therefore not comparable. Latency and throughput were measured on one device for every model under half-precision autocasting. Latency is the mean $\pm$ standard deviation of 100 timed single-image forward passes after 10 warm-up passes; throughput is measured at batch size 32 as the mean over 20 timed passes after 3 warm-up passes, for which per-pass timings were not retained. ``Acc./M'' is test accuracy in percentage points per million parameters. Rows are ordered by parameter count.
