\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
\item Six 3D CNN and transformer networks trained under one protocol on BraTS 2020.
\item UNet reaches the highest mean Dice of 0.8199 at native resolution.
\item CNNs beat every transformer-based model; all pairwise differences are significant.
\item VNet reaches 97\% of the UNet Dice with 1.40 million parameters.
\item Enhancing-tumour Dice on low-grade gliomas falls to 0.16--0.42.
\end{itemize}
