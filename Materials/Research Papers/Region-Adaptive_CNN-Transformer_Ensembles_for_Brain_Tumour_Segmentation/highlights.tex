\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
\item Per-region weights and thresholds fuse nnU-Net, SegResNet and Swin UNETR-v2.
\item Tested on 56 internal BraTS 2020 and 60 external BraTS-Africa patients.
\item The ensemble beats two members but not SegResNet alone (Dice 0.8530 vs 0.8717).
\item Member disagreement flags failing cases: AUROC 0.993 internal, 0.870 external.
\item Scored Seg-Grad-CAM shows near-chance attribution for the transformer.
\end{itemize}
