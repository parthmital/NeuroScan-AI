Effective batch & 64 images: micro-batches of 64, or of 32 accumulated over two steps for Swin-Base, the largest that fitted on one GPU \\
Optimiser steps & Equal for every model on a given fold: 260 per binary detection run (13 per epoch), and 1,420, 1,240, 1,260, 1,200 and 780 on subtype classification folds 0 to 4, whose training sets differ in size \\
Optimiser & AdamW, learning rate $3\times 10^{-4}$, weight decay 0.01 \\
Learning rate schedule & Linear warm-up, then cosine decay per optimiser step \\
Loss & Binary cross-entropy with logits (binary detection) and cross-entropy (subtype classification) \\
Precision & Half-precision autocasting with gradient scaling \\
Gradient clipping & Norm 1.0 \\
Checkpoint selection & Lowest validation log-loss (a strictly proper scoring rule), evaluated after every epoch \\
Cross-validation & 5 group-aware folds plus a fixed held-out test set \\
Runs per task & 45 full-image runs and 9 background-only runs, all 54 completed, with 63.5 minutes of training for binary detection and 264.6 for subtype classification \\
Random seed & 42, with deterministic kernels not enforced, so reruns reproduce the figures closely but not bit for bit \\
Calibration & Temperature fitted per run on its validation fold over $\log T\in[-3, 3]$, with binary detection reported at threshold 0.5 and at the mean validation-tuned Youden threshold \\
Uncertainty & Bootstrap 95\% confidence intervals of accuracy and of the accuracy gap to the leading model. A gap interval that includes zero cannot separate a model from the leader \\
Attention maps & Grad-CAM at the last spatial or token stage (the last convolution for VGG16) of the fold model with the lowest validation loss. Occlusion sensitivity computed identically for every model on a common stratified test sample, because Grad-CAM maps of convolutional and attention stages are not strictly commensurable \\
Efficiency & Latency over 100 single-image passes after 10 warm-up passes, and throughput at batch size 32 over 20 passes after 3 warm-up passes, each repeated three times. Training time, throughput and peak memory averaged over the folds \\
