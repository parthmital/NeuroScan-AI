\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
\item Nine backbones trained with one pipeline, equal steps and five-fold cross-validation.
\item Deduplication and group-aware splits leave no test image near a training image.
\item Top binary detection models reach 99.42\% but none differs significantly.
\item Background-only models beat chance for 16 of 18 model-task pairs.
\item EfficientNetB0 matches the leaders with 4.01 million parameters.
\end{itemize}
